User Manual

Virtual Tour & Cultural Mapping — CALABARZON. A guided walk through every screen, plus installation and answers to common questions.
Version 1.1.1 · Android, iOS (Expo Go) and web demo
Screens are shown on a phone-sized display; the Android app looks the same apart from native maps.
Live web demo: clarkdesepida0154.github.io/virtual-tour-calabarzon

Installing the app

Android — APK (recommended)

  1. On your phone open the project's Releases page on GitHub: github.com/clarkdesepida0154/virtual-tour-calabarzon/releases/latest.
  2. Download virtual-tour-calabarzon-v1.1.1.apk (about 127 MB).
  3. Open the file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager, then tap Install.
  4. Open Virtual Tour & Cultural Mapping. An internet connection is needed the first time.

The APK is a sideloaded build, not from Google Play. If you still have v1.0.0, uninstall it first — it had a launch problem fixed in v1.0.1. v1.1.1 installs over v1.1.0 and v1.0.1.

Expo Go (Android or iOS)

  1. Install Expo Go from the Play Store or App Store.
  2. On a computer with Node.js 20+: clone the repository, run npm install, copy .env.example to .env and fill it in, then run npx expo start.
  3. Scan the QR code with Expo Go (same Wi-Fi; use npx expo start --tunnel if it cannot connect).

Web demo

Open clarkdesepida0154.github.io/virtual-tour-calabarzon in a modern browser for a quick look; see the FAQ for its limits.

Screen-by-screen guide

1. First launch — onboarding

On a true first launch three slides introduce the app: virtual tours, learn & quiz, and your journey.

  • Next moves forward, Skip jumps out, Get started on the last slide continues.
  • It is shown once and never again.

2. Welcome, sign in and guest mode

Choose Create an account, Sign in or Continue as guest. Guests have every feature; progress is stored on the phone.

  • Sign-up and sign-in check email and password and explain problems in plain language.
  • If a guest creates an account later, visits, scores, favorites and ratings move over automatically.
  • Sign out (Progress tab) returns to Welcome.

3. Home

The hero shows the app name and a search field; typing and submitting opens Explore with that search. Below: the Featured carousel (varied, rotating daily), cards for the planner and Heritage Insights, a category grid and province shortcuts. The gear opens Settings.

4. Explore — search, filters and Near me

  • Type in the search bar to filter by name or town as you type.
  • Tap a category chip and a province chip — they combine. Tap the active chip again to clear it.
  • Cards show the photo, name, town • province, category, a heart (favorite) and, when rated, the ★ average.
  • Near me: tap Allow location (or the Nearest first chip) to show distances and sort the nearest first. The app never asks on launch; if you decline, the feature simply hides. Your position stays on the phone.
  • Pull down to refresh. The round + button adds a destination.

5. Destination details

  • Cover photo, name, tags, Visited and best-score badges, and the address with Get directions (opens Google Maps), View in 360° (opens the tour's in-app 360° view, where a panorama exists) and Open in Google Maps (Street View in Google Maps, for every place).
  • Top bar: back, share, favorite.
  • Description, a map preview (tap to open the Map tab there), History and Cultural Significance, and the photo credit.
  • Rate this place with 1–5 stars; change it any time. The average and count are shown.
  • The bottom bar holds Start Virtual Tour and Quiz. Manage this entry offers Edit and Delete (with confirmation).

6. Add and edit a destination

Use the + button on Explore (or Edit on Details). Name, description, address, province and category are required; coordinates are optional. Pick a photo — it is resized and uploaded for you. After saving, a starter quiz is generated from your entry and marked “Auto-generated starter quiz”; edit the text and it regenerates.

7. Virtual tour, narration and 360°

  • Full-screen and sequential: Intro → Gallery → 360° View → History → Cultural Significance → Completion. Use the arrows or swipe; the Android back button steps back; ✕ closes.
  • The gallery credits every photograph.
  • 360° View (25 of the 26 places): Google Street View right inside the tour — drag to look around, pinch to zoom. It loads with a soft shimmer; Open in Google Maps continues in Google Maps. Places without a panorama skip this step (their gallery keeps a link to Google Maps instead), and it is hidden while offline.
  • On the chapters tap Listen to hear the text read aloud (speed in Settings). It stops when you move on, close the tour or leave the app. The button is hidden when the phone has no English voice.
  • At the end tap Mark as Visited, then Take the Quiz or open directions to visit in person.

8. Chapters

History (Chapter I) and Cultural Significance (Chapter II) are set on cream “paper” with a gold-ruled lead sentence. The Listen button pulses while narrating.

9. Quiz

  • Five questions, four options each, shuffled every attempt. Tap an answer for instant feedback: green with a check for correct, terracotta with a cross for wrong (colour is never the only cue).
  • The score screen shows a ring with X/5 and %, with a celebration at 4/5 or better. Retake as often as you like; your best score is kept and shown on Details.

10. Progress and badges

  • The Progress tab shows who you are (account or guest), an N/26 ring, quizzes taken, average and best score, per-province bars, your favorites and a “Your journey” list.
  • Badges: ten achievements (First Journey, a badge per province, Heritage Scholar, Quiz Enthusiast, Collector, CALABARZON Master). Earned ones show their date; locked ones show how far along you are. A banner announces a new badge and never blocks what you are doing.

11. Certificate of Completion

Visit every destination of a province to unlock its certificate; visit all 26 for the CALABARZON Heritage Explorer certificate. Open it from Progress or the matching badge, type your name (it is remembered) and tap Share to send the certificate as an image. It keeps its parchment look in light and dark themes.

12. Ratings and Heritage Insights

Ratings appear on Details and as ★ averages on Explore cards. Heritage Insights (Home card or the chart icon on Progress) shows community totals, the most visited and top-rated places, the toughest quizzes, and visits by province and category.

13. Field trip planner

Open from Home or the route button on the Map. Tick up to 8 stops, optionally start from your location, and the app suggests the shortest order. The map draws the route along real roads with the real driving distance and time for every leg and in total (for example “38.7 km drive · ~52 min”). Reorder with the arrows and the route updates. Without a connection — or if the routing service is busy — the map shows straight dashed lines and approximate figures instead (right). Open the route in Google Maps, and save plans on the phone: a saved plan keeps its road route, also offline. Routing © OSRM / OpenStreetMap.

14. Map

All 26 destinations as category-coloured pins. The chip row shows counts and doubles as a filter; tap a pin for a preview card and open it for the details. The corner button zooms back to the whole region. (The browser demo shows a schematic map; the phone app shows Google Maps, with a night style in dark mode.)

15. Settings, language and theme

  • Appearance: System, Light or Dark — applied immediately.
  • Language: English or Filipino, applied immediately. Destination write-ups and quizzes remain English.
  • Narration: show or hide the Listen button; Normal or Slow speed.
  • About (version, source code) and Legal (Photo credits, open-source note).

16. Dark mode and Filipino interface

Every screen has a dark theme on deep warm browns, and every label, message and screen-reader description exists in everyday Filipino (for example “Tuklasin”, “Mga Paborito”).

17. Offline behaviour

Without a connection the app shows the last loaded destinations with an Offline — showing saved data banner and a Retry button. Searching and filtering still work on the saved list. Actions that need the internet (saving, rating, directions, 360°, road routing) are hidden or explain themselves. Certificates and saved trip plans work offline. Photo credits (right) are listed for all 78 photographs.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Choose Continue as guest. An account only lets you keep your progress if you change phones or reinstall.

I created an account and my progress disappeared.

Progress made as a guest on the same phone moves to the account at sign-up. Progress made as a guest on another device stays on that device.

Why did the app not ask for my location at launch?

By design: it asks only when you tap Allow location or Nearest first, and uses it only to compute distances on your phone.

The Listen button is missing.

Your phone has no English text-to-speech voice, or narration is turned off in Settings → Narration. Install an English voice in the system text-to-speech settings.

Why is there no 360° step for some places?

Each of the 26 places was checked: 25 have a Google Street View panorama nearby and get the in-tour 360° View; the one without (Taal Volcano & Lake) keeps its 5-step tour. Open in Google Maps works everywhere; where Google has no panorama it shows the map.

A place I added shows “No 360° imagery here yet”.

Places you add get the 360° step optimistically. If Google has no panorama near them, the view stays dark and this note appears after a few seconds.

The route on the planner is a dashed straight line.

Road routing needs the internet. Offline or when the routing service does not answer, the planner falls back to straight lines and approximate distances.

Can I use it offline?

Yes, for places you have already opened (see “Offline behaviour”). Quizzes, visits and ratings need a connection to save.

Why are the descriptions only in English?

The interface is bilingual; the destination write-ups and quizzes are English for now.

Where do the photographs come from?

Wikimedia Commons, under free licences. Each is credited in the tour and under Settings → Photo credits.

What can the web demo not do?

The map is a schematic stand-in, there is no haptic feedback, narration depends on the browser's voices, and the certificate shares as text rather than an image.

The APK will not install.

It is about 127 MB because it contains the app for all processor types. Free some storage and retry, and make sure v1.0.0 is uninstalled.
